Change

The delegate gains an optional bounded runtime-seconds value for native,
non-copy jobs. It accepts an integer from 1 through 900 and validates before
starting systemd. Boolean values and other types are rejected by the builder.
Legacy limits remain 120 seconds for non-native, 240 seconds for native, and
900 seconds for native copy-engine jobs. Resource limits and environment
allowlisting remain unchanged.

Only world-application-control requests 600 seconds in the delegate and sets
CTest TIMEOUT to 660 seconds. The extra 60 seconds allows service cleanup.
Actor operation deadlines, other registrations, harness identifiers, locked
acceptance data, schemas, and production actor behavior are unchanged.
