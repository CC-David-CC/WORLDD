Production blockers left untouched

That helper rejects the same flag. Resource-backed native broker startup therefore fails during private bootstrap. The owned-broker and broker-death tests cannot be repaired safely by changing fixture expectations or removing resource/native startup. A production fix and rebuilt package are required.

A new test also verifies that a caller-supplied private bootstrap option is rejected before scope ownership.
