Scope and reason

The production repair distinguishes live actor authority from revoked actor identity.
A broker ActorRef contains issuer and actor identifiers. It contains no credential.
Credential invalidation belongs to the authenticated world-session boundary.
The broker must not label a valid actor's identity revoked merely because any revocation occurred.

The existing docs/CONTRACT.md:90 requires UNAUTHORIZED for unknown or revoked actor tokens.
It requires FORBIDDEN for a missing grant. The root AGENTS.md also separates identity and authority.
The cabinet pre-dispatch ancestor race requires FORBIDDEN and a usable requester session.
The presentation ancestor race requires FORBIDDEN or UNAVAILABLE, with no protected observation.
Those two failing expectations remain unchanged.

Three legacy fence controls used the former blanket code/message.
This change corrects their expected classification while retaining strict refusal and safety assertions.
It is explicitly versioned separately from the production repair.
It does not broaden an error allowlist to arbitrary failures or turn failures into skips.
