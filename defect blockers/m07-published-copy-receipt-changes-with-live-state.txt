copy_destination_inspect reconstructs a published receipt instead of returning its stored receipt.
Repeated copy_destination_prepare also reconstructs that receipt.
destination_receipt reads the current destination state_mapping_revision.
A later configured-state quota reassignment can increase that revision.
The same completed copy operation can then return different receipt bytes.
The client requires the original fresh publication revision and can fence the owner channel.
This conclusion comes from source review. A runtime reproducer remains pending.

Return the exact durable published receipt after validating its allocation and publication bindings.
Inspect current mutable state separately before granting live directory access.
Do not reconstruct historical publication evidence from mutable state.
Test exact retry and inspection after destination state use, reassignment, and restart.
Require unchanged original receipt bytes and unchanged operation identity.
Keep missing or inconsistent receipts uncertain. Do not invent a successful historical receipt.
