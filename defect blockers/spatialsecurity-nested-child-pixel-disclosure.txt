SpatialSecurity nested-child pixel disclosure

Confirmed on sb16 and sb16-2 against source a89ec13.
Status: correction passes focused checks on both hosts.

An application owns a parent and its child. A privileged service places a foreign window below that child.
GetImage on the parent exposes the foreign grandchild's pixels.
Native and swapped clients reproduce the same disclosure.
The baseline passes 16 other pixel checks and fails these two added checks on each host.

Cause:
The policy unions all authorized descendant borderClip regions after subtracting foreign regions.
An authorized ancestor's borderClip includes foreign descendants.
The final union restores those foreign pixels.

Proposed correction:
Traverse ancestors before descendants. Apply each window's authority to the region in tree order.
Subtract foreign regions. Restore authorized descendant regions. Clip the result to the original parent region.
Keep traversal iterative and fail closed on region allocation errors.

This defect is in the project's SpatialSecurity policy. It does not require a new upstream XACE hook.
The regression preserves owned pixels and authorized grandchildren within foreign children.
Evidence: spatial-os-luna-handoff/evidence/m04-nested-pixels-2026-10-11.

Validation:

All 18 pixel cases pass, including both reproduced failures and the nested positive controls.
Server unit checks pass. No sanitizer finding appears in the collected logs.
