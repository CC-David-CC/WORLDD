Distributed revocation requirement, 2026-10-09

Status: missing requirement reproduced with real production processes.
This is a Spatial-M1 authority requirement. It is separate from the XLibre timer patches.

The issuer currently confirms that it saved a revocation.
Worlds receive committed events through a separate TLS watcher.
That watcher does not acknowledge completed world or native-input cancellation to the issuer.
The current reply therefore does not establish a distributed cancellation barrier.
docs/WORLD_IDENTITY.md already records this limitation.

This diagnostic demonstrates the gap. It does not count as passing M02 acceptance.

Required next behavior:
Track authenticated world and workspace authority participants.
Keep committed revocation separate from completed cancellation.
Wait for each obligated authority to confirm its actual cancellation effects.
Keep unavailable or disconnected participants unresolved until safe fencing or recovery is confirmed.
Do not acknowledge only because an event was received or a network timeout expired.
Preserve independently authorized applications and independent grants.
Test real native input release and process termination with independent witnesses.
