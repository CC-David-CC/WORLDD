Departure is an additive WorldSession operation. Existing close semantics are unchanged.
A request needs the authenticated source session, a fresh actor sequence, and a 32-byte transition identity.
The world consumes the sequence and closes the actor session. Further source actions are unauthorized.
The actor remains reserved until outer-scheduler cancellation settles and its context save completes.
Only that actor is checkpointed. Workspace selection already belongs to the world database.
The reply includes transition identity, world identity, actor identity, and resulting world revision.
A context-save failure returns an error after departure; it does not claim rollback or safe destination activation.
Connection loss suppresses the reply but retains pending cleanup and save while the authority remains running.
An unreceived receipt is uncertain. No retry or cross-host atomicity is claimed.

Destination preparation extension:
Authentication begin may carry a 32-byte transition identity. Successful signed authentication returns prepared instead of opened.
Prepared authentication retains the existing configured bootstrap behavior; it does not invent grants or bypass admission.
No actor controller or actor geometry is created by preparation. Actions and inspection remain unauthorized.
The preparation expires after 60 seconds; this is a reservation lifetime, not an operation delay.
Activate must match its transition identity and recheck credential status and admission.
Activation consumes the preparation. Replayed activation cannot create another controller.
Close, transport loss and credential revocation discard preparation.
A world-policy change is checked again at activation, even if preparation previously succeeded.
Spawn validation and controller conflicts may still reject activation; a coordinator must report this post-departure failure honestly.
