Primary review:
- Bindings require the authenticated parent holder and ordinary scope, rights, and depth attenuation.
- Issuer templates cannot create domain roots.
- A binding records immutable issuer, exact profile revision, template, parent, and checked terms.
- Revoking a binding stops new materialization. Existing grants retain their own ancestry.
- Receipts exclude credential and connection identity. Profile revision cannot bypass an existing receipt.
- Grant and receipt insertion share a domain transaction. Receipt failure rolls back the grant and revision.
- Exact retries return the original grant, including revocation. They do not authorize actions.
- Missing or mismatched receipt targets fail instead of creating replacement grants.
- Parent revocation invalidates dependent grants. Independent grants remain active.
- The explicit schema-one-to-two migration preserves authority identity, grants, revocations, and revision.
- Production migration refuses live stores and wrong domains. Failed migration rolls back schema changes.
- Fixture probe operations forward to the store. They do not authenticate actors or replace runtime endpoints.
