Original evidence
evidence/m04-gate-2026-10-11/crosshost-primary-review.txt records eight passed scenarios.
Those runs cover delayed and crashed authority participants in both directions and profiles.
They verify native input release, surviving applications, exact reservation cleanup,
issuer acknowledgement or retirement, and the four-obligation revocation barrier.
They are historical executions. This review does not relabel them as executions of M05 binaries.

Source comparison
m04-crosshost-source-comparison.json compares 109 relevant files.
The identity, supervisor, broker/platform, X11, renderer implementation, and XLibre paths are unchanged.
The cross-host fixture, application fixture, storage fixture, and its transport schema are unchanged.
The only changed file in that set is src/world/actors.cpp.
Its change copies live actor entities into a snapshot used by Actors::scene.
It does not change cancellation, grant refresh, input release, control routing, or authority retirement.
The separate src/core/scene.cpp change renders other actors as markers.
The additive core.proto migration supplies actor marker configuration and the RenderScene actor list.
These rendering changes are covered by the new local and cross-host actor-visibility checks.

Affected-path review
SharedSession in crosshost_revocation.py starts one actor per independently authoritative world.
held_application sends real input to its confined application through the existing actor route.
The scene excludes the observing actor's own marker. These fixtures do not add another actor to that world.
Cancellation checks read the native witness and actual retained reservations.
Crash recovery checks production cleanup and issuer retirement receipts, not scene pixels.
The new test-only optional replacement listener is unused by this earlier matrix.
Its production validation path has separate positive and negative cross-host recovery coverage.

Decision
Retain the eight prior scenarios as evidence for their unchanged M04 cancellation and retirement contracts.
Require the full frozen M05 local matrix and new frozen M05 cross-host layer before qualification.
Do not use this carry-forward decision as evidence of shared persistent-world behavior.
That behavior requires the topology, remote-key, shared-state, and supervised-recovery cases.
Any failure in the new gate that affects these unchanged paths reopens this decision.
M05 is not qualified by this document.
