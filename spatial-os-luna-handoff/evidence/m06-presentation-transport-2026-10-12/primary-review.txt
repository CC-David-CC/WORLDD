Primary review: private presentation transport
Status: passed focused verification on sb16 and sb16-2 in Debug and ASan-UBSan.

Reviewed behavior
PresentationStream owns an authenticated UNIX stream socket and close-on-exec transferred descriptors.
It validates the socket creator separately from the kernel credentials on every received chunk.
The first receive reads exactly one byte, so later descriptor injection cannot be mistaken for a valid first-byte handoff.
The transport permits at most two descriptors per frame and closes delivered descriptors after malformed or truncated ancillary input.
Outgoing descriptors are duplicated before queueing and released after the first successful send.
Partial sends never resend ancillary descriptors.
The maximum payload is 1 MiB. Output allows at most eight frames and 2 MiB including framing.
Each receive call has a bounded work budget. Each flush writes at most 64 KiB.
Input and output deadlines are fixed at five seconds. Partial progress does not refresh them.
Read/framing/credential failures and output failures close the socket and clear owned pending state.
Callers must invoke receive and flush on periodic event-loop ticks, including when readiness is absent, to enforce deadlines.
The transport carries descriptors; its caller must still validate sealed configuration, process identity, operation schema, and authority.

Tests and evidence
Eleven tests passed in every host/profile: 44 final check executions with no failures or skips.
Tests cover protobuf framing, two sealed descriptors, excessive descriptors, ancillary truncation, and descriptor injection after the first byte.
They cover wrong socket creators and another process using an inherited descriptor.
They cover excessive/truncated frames, a partial-progress input deadline, and a stalled-reader output deadline.
The probe verifies descriptor counts after rejection and uses the production transport for all I/O.
Audits verify 431 source files on both hosts and the local reviewed worktree, locked acceptance hashes, probe binary hashes, and retained logs.
No sanitizer findings appeared in the retained test or build logs.
Full artifacts remain under local/m06-presentation-transport-03 in each isolated M06 host checkout.

Limits

The control/world-channel loop, retained graphics ownership, supervisor receipt integration, and complete actor world transitions remain pending.
This focused transport result does not qualify M06 or later milestones.
