## Production composition blocker found during review

Source review identifies two prerequisites for the live-worker SIGKILL recovery oracle.
The old and new broker roots must share one stable delegated parent, while retaining
separate per-generation ProcessScope roots. FileEngine must also receive actual drain
proof before the supervisor removes the recorded worker scope.

Keep the existing pre-prune callback design. The lifecycle owner supplies a callback to
the exact ProcessScope cleanup operation; that callback records the checked drain before
pruning. Recovery may then consume that persisted identity. An absent scope alone remains
insufficient proof, and the worktree stays quarantined when exact cleanup is uncertain.

Relevant source: src/workspace/file_engine.cpp and src/supervisor/process_scope.cpp.
Relevant fixture: tests/native/copy_fault_supervised_world.py. The prototype retains
CANCELLED, exact staging removal, and successful follow-up assertions. Do not weaken them.
Do not edit issuer state or copy-job records to manufacture drain proof. This fixture prototype makes no production changes and does not add a protobuf attestation.
