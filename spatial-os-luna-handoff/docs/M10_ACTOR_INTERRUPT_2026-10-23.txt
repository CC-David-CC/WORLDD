M10 actor transport interruption
Date: 2026-10-23. Status: IMPLEMENTING, UNQUALIFIED_M10.

Physical input loss must not wait behind a blocked actor exchange.
The owner thread may obtain a weak ActorSessionInterrupt handle after authentication.
The handle owns a CLOEXEC duplicate of that exact actor socket.
A watchdog may request shutdown without acquiring the actor-operation mutex.
The descriptor stays pinned for the handle's lifetime. It cannot retarget a reused descriptor.
The client reports control unavailable after interruption and never retries the action.
Client failure and destruction shut down retained duplicates before closing the primary channel.
Other actors, worlds, applications, and their execution authority remain independent.

A shutdown request is not a native cancellation acknowledgement.
Earlier effects may remain uncertain. Require independent world/native evidence before claiming input release.
Normal input denials use the separate checked-cancellation outcome when worldd confirms release.
Interrupted transport requires explicit recovery. Do not reconnect and replay it automatically.

The new API is an in-process cancellation handle. Actor wire protocols are unchanged by this increment.
Selected tests before execution:
actor-control-interrupt: blocked read, partial blocked write, idle interruption, and retained-handle lifetime.
actor-session-client and actor-session-client-tls: all existing framing, identity, ownership, timeout, and no-replay regressions.
Native disconnect, actor-retirement, release-order, and application-survival evidence remain mandatory for M10 integration.
