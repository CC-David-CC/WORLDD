The new --xi2-receipts flag explicitly enables XI2 and additional receipt records.
Existing core mode and plain --xi2 retain their output fields and behavior.
Receipt readiness adds xi2_receipts=true. The existing xi2=true field remains required.
Each received XI2 key, button, or motion emits one xi2_receipt before its legacy event record.
Receipt fields: receipt_ordinal, event_type, deviceid, sourceid, xid, detail, time, synthetic.
receipt_ordinal starts at1 and increases across these input events for the probe process lifetime.
Ordinal exhaustion terminates the fixture rather than wrapping.
time preserves the native event's unsigned32-bit X timestamp. It is not a monotonic ordering oracle.
synthetic reports Xlib send_event. Do not accept synthetic receipt records as native actor input.
Focus events retain existing output. They do not receive the new receipt record.

The supervisor must associate the process and X connection with its authenticated session and arm.
The observer must pin client, window, and device lifetimes independently.
Receipt fields alone do not establish those identities or reject stale pre-arm events.
The new reader must reject missing, ambiguous, mismatched, replayed, and later replacement receipts.
The receipt proves only what this independent X client received.
It does not prove historical native down state, balanced release, or destroyed-window event delivery.
Pair it with the server-side snapshot at the first qualifying peer event.
