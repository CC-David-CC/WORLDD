Application-control recovery: baseline and next invariant
Status: historical baseline. Both corrected Debug diagnostics reproduced the original reservation gap.
Read CURRENT_STATE.md and tasks/m1/identity.control_recovery.json for subsequent implementation and qualification evidence.

Required behavior

A trusted supervisor must release a crashed generation's control reservations after it verifies native cancellation.
Preserve applications whose execution authority remains valid.
Keep unrelated reservations and independently valid grants.
Keep issuer retirement pending until this dependent cleanup is confirmed.

Historical source findings

ControlLeases stores a lease ID, actor principal, application reference, and revocation flag.
It has no owning world generation or recovery scope.
Repeated acquisition by the same actor returns its existing lease.
Another actor receives BUSY until the reservation is released.
Revocation retains the reservation until the trusted world confirms native cancellation through release.
These rules correctly prevent premature control transfer during ordinary revocation.

The current private broker channel has one trusted caller.
BrokerClient closes its descriptor when destroyed. It does not own the broker process.
The broker stops its applications when that private management channel reaches EOF.
The diagnostic fixture can retain the broker endpoint while the world exits.
Retaining an endpoint is not a production recovery implementation.

The combined294 supervisor record has checked process and native cleanup receipts.
It has no dependent control-reservation receipt.
OWNED_SCOPES_STOPPED therefore does not establish complete authority recovery.
Do not interpret that stage as permission to retire issuer obligations.

Next implementation constraints

Bind reservations to an immutable recovery owner before admitting application control.
Derive that owner from the retained exact launch. Do not accept an actor-selected owner.
Retain broker communication independently of the crashed world's descriptor.
Stop that owner's new acquisitions before checking native cleanup.
Account for acquisition committed by the broker when its reply was lost.
A list of replies observed by the world is insufficient evidence of all reservations.
Release only reservations owned by the exact fenced generation.
Require a checked broker response, or checked cleanup by that broker's registered recovery supervisor.
Treat missing replies, wrong generations, and uncertain ownership as unresolved.
Persist the checked dependent receipt before issuer retirement.
Do not kill independently authorized applications to obtain an empty reservation table.

The additive protobuf interface and its ownership checks require a separate implementation review.
No existing acceptance assertion or locked contract changes in this baseline.
