M10 tap-rate requirement change, 2026-10-28

David changed the required sustained rate from 250 to 100 key taps per second.
The application witness and independent native witness each retain a separate
100-tap-per-second minimum. The same batches, ordering, event counts, device
identity, and delivery assertions remain required.

This replaces the 250-tap floor described in earlier M10 documents. Historical
measurements and failure reports remain evidence of their original runs. They
do not establish a pass under this revised requirement. The changed candidate
must pass the required tests on both hosts and the full frozen-candidate gate.
