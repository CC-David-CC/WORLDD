Failure and invariant

The native storage broker fixture failed on both qualification hosts after a
normal broker shutdown. `ProcessScope` retired its drained broker cgroup, so
`assert_owned_scopes_empty_for_state` tried to read `cgroup.events` from a
removed path. `ProcessScope::stop` removes a scope only after checking the
pinned cgroup identity and confirming no processes remain. Its cleanup also
removes empty child cgroups first.

The fixture now accepts absence only for the exact broker identity read from
the state's `.broker-instance-id`. Before that check, it verifies the pinned
delegated cgroup and broker-root device/inode identities. Existing scopes
must remain real directories with empty `cgroup.events`, empty `cgroup.procs`,
and no symlinks or child cgroups. Permission and other I/O errors propagate.

The fixture records the exact regular kernel-interface names in the new broker
root after enabling controllers and before spawning any broker. It requires
the enabled cpu, memory, pids, and io interfaces. The baseline rejects symlinks,
child directories, and non-kernel names. Later scans accept regular files only
from that captured set. They reject new entries, symlinks, malformed scope
names, and foreign scope identities. The same-boot parent-change, legacy-registry,
replacement-filesystem, crash/restart, peer-survival, and exact diagnostic
assertions remain. Live-scope population checks remain strict.
