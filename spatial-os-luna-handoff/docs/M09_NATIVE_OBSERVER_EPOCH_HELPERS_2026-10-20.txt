Additional actual native facts

SnapshotDevices() copies these facts in the same callback-local pass as the complete native down/postdown arrays:
- deviceGrab.grab != NullGrab on every master/slave device, without dereferencing the grab.
- deviceGrab.sync.frozen, sync.other != NullGrab, and the unmodified native sync.state integer.
- Pending QdEventRec count for each freshly resolved device, using callback-local identity comparison only.
- Whether syncEvents.replayDev identifies that device.
- Presence of a focus class, its native revert value, and classification by real focus sentinels.
The snapshot also records global pending-queue presence, replayDev presence, and syncEvents.playingEvents.
The qualification-only SPATIAL_OBSERVER_MAX_PENDING_RECORDS cap is 1024 total queue records per snapshot.
One global pass counts every record, including records unrelated to the four pinned devices.
It performs at most 1024 record classifications and four device comparisons per classified record.
Finding another queue record after that budget fails collection before reading its device field.
Exactly 1024 records may be captured; 1025 or more never produce a valid snapshot.
The caller retains valid == FALSE and the initial nonzero bitmap sentinels on failure.
The cap never truncates evidence into success. It imposes no production queue limit or input-policy change.
Missing keyboard focus classes remain invalid.
A missing old device never becomes an empty/clear record.

FocusClassRec.win is compared with NoneWin, PointerRootWin, and FollowKeyboardWin from pinned include/input.h.
All other values classify as OTHER. OTHER does not assert that the pointer names a live window.
The helper never dereferences a focus window, saved grab, sync.other, replayDev, or saved sync.event pointer.
A pointer to saved sync.event is not a pending-input fact: devices allocate that buffer during creation.
The helper traverses the server-owned pending queue synchronously and copies counts. It retains no queue/device pointer.
The focus enum is a helper classification, not a native wire enum or a release-success flag.
NOT_CAPTURED means no focus class was observed; it cannot satisfy a required keyboard-focus check.
