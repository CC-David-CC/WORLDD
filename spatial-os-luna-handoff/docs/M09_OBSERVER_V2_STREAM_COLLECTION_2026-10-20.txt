Descriptor ownership

Cleanup semantics

The collector temporarily makes blocking descriptors nonblocking.
Cleanup closes the selector, restores the original blocking mode, and attempts descriptor closure.
Each cleanup step still runs after an earlier cleanup OSError.
An existing transport or semantic exception keeps its original identity.
Otherwise, cleanup errors raise SELECTOR_CLOSE_FAILED, FD_RESTORE_FAILED, or FD_CLOSE_FAILED.
The first cleanup error takes precedence when several cleanup steps fail.
The collector never retries close, including Linux EINTR.
A cleanup failure cannot turn an otherwise successful collection into PASS.
