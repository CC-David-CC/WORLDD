M10 interruption capability and retained actor context
Date: 2026-10-23. Status: IMPLEMENTING, UNQUALIFIED_M10.

A rejected world transition may retain the same authenticated source channel in a new host generation.
An old watchdog must not interrupt that retained channel after a new context becomes active.
ActorSessionClient.rotate_interrupt_handle revokes the old in-process interruption capability.
It sends no actor action and does not reconnect, authenticate again, or replay prior input.
The new handle still pins the same exact actor socket.

Request and retirement use a separate small handle mutex. Neither acquires the actor-operation mutex.
If interruption wins the handoff, rotation fails and control remains fenced.
If retirement wins, later old-handle requests have no effect on the new context.
Client failure always shuts down its primary socket before closing descriptors.
A retained retired duplicate therefore cannot keep a failed actor channel alive.
Wire messages, authority, legacy mutating errors, and cancellation acknowledgements remain unchanged.
