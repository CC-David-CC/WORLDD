M08 copy fixture I/O closure repair (2026-10-19)

Cause

The legacy file-action fixture creates a private storage volume, then loads the full-gate ambient broker resource configuration. It previously added the fixture device only to selected aggregate and default-policy budgets. Explicit workspace policies retained ambient devices, so the broker rejected the policy before startup: Resource policies must cover the same I/O devices. BrokenPipe and stable-delegation-parent failures are cascades.

Repair

Read the protected StorageVolumeRuntime and nested owner configuration for the created fixture volume. Verify volume root, filesystem identity, mount inspection, and loop receipt. Use the owner budget's complete loop and backing-device closure to rebind every nested ProcessScopeBudget. Preserve non-I/O fields and cap existing I/O rates by fixture owner limits. Reject empty, invalid, or duplicate closure entries before mutation. Do not change production budget intersection or acceptance oracles.
