Why M01 remains open

The integrated world and workspace services do not yet use ParticipantRuntime.
Their issuer notification and current-status checks cannot alone prove completed distributed cancellation.
The retained delayed-delivery diagnostic observed actor motion after the issuer's durable commit reply.
Motion stopped after the real event arrived.
That diagnostic does not mean cryptographic enrollment is missing.
It identifies the integration dependency shared with M02.

Finish the participant and supervisor path before signing off the full milestone.
Then require delayed delivery, blocked native cleanup, failed authorities, and cross-host cancellation evidence.
Keep issuer commit, cancellation completion, and recovery as separate reported results.
No completion percentage follows from the number of tests or prepared patches.
