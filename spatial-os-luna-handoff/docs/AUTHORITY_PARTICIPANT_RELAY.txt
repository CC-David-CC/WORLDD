Supervisor participant relay
Status: isolated candidate01 passed focused Debug on both hosts. Sanitizer qualification is running.

Invariant

The supervisor forwards exact child commands through its retained authenticated participant connection.
Only the owned child receives the inherited channel endpoint.
Transport receipt does not acknowledge completed child-side cancellation.

Launch and ownership

Prepare the private channel only for a live supervised launch with durable approval.
Require authentication and an initial revocation baseline before channel preparation.
Return child configuration with the exact issuer, participant, registration, and service incarnation.
Do not include TLS configuration or private-key paths.
Reserve one child descriptor between 3 and 255.
Reject duplicate targets and collisions with other inherited descriptors.
Pass the child endpoint only through the owned ProcessScope startup gate.
Close the parent's child-endpoint copy after the start attempt.
Do not reconstruct channel ownership after restart or retry execution after uncertain startup.

Forwarding

Use bounded, nonblocking ParticipantCommand and ParticipantUpdate frames.
The supervisor event loop pumps the channel through AuthorityLaunch.
Retain authenticated bootstrap updates in order until they can reach the child.
Disable the external participant poll and command paths once the relay owns consumption.
Before accepting child commands, check the retained process scope and direct child lifetime.
Forward one consequential child command at a time.
Preserve the request ID, command payload, and worker update ordering.
Do not manufacture authentication, activation, acknowledgement, or release results.
Do not automatically activate, acknowledge, release, reconnect, or replay.

Revision races

The parent's received revision can exceed the child's consumed revision.
An explicitly forwarded activation may name an earlier received revision.
It cannot name a revision beyond the parent's received feed.
Forward the child's revision unchanged. The real issuer still requires its exact current revision.
Return the issuer's revision conflict to the child for explicit synchronization and retry.
Keep the direct ParticipantRuntime submit contract unchanged.
Applied acknowledgements retain their contiguous revision and active-generation checks.
Parent receipt and channel writes never complete a revocation barrier.

Failure

Malformed commands, queue overflow, channel failure, and child exit permanently disable relay routing.
Close the channel and participant connection. Preserve unresolved issuer generations and obligations.
The supervisor must perform checked process and native cleanup separately.
A successful release closes the relay only after its reply has drained.
Cleanup and restart do not infer issuer retirement.
