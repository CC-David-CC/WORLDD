Role and producer boundaries
----------------------------
The schema wire roles are SUPERVISOR=1, BROKER=2, COMPOSITOR=3,
APPLICATION=4. The serializer native constants are APPLICATION=1,
SUPERVISOR=2, COMPOSITOR=3. Use its checked explicit mapping:
APPLICATION 1 -> wire APPLICATION 4; SUPERVISOR 2 -> wire SUPERVISOR 1;
COMPOSITOR 3 -> wire COMPOSITOR 3. Reject unknown values. Equal integers do
not imply equal roles.

The server-origin serializer emits exactly three records. The fourth
ProbeReceiptBatch must come from an independent probe producer. The server must
not fabricate that receipt. A later stream assembler may join independently
validated records, but cannot create missing evidence. The trusted worldd seat
bindings also remain independent input on their own endpoint.

One-shot and ownership invariants
---------------------------------
* Only the authorized launch owner may create and pass the endpoint. The
  receiving process must be the verified intended XLibre server child.
* The kernel-held endpoint conveys authority; session, arm, role, XID, and epoch
  fields are validation/join data only.
* Keep CLOEXEC as the default. Clear it only for the verified target descriptor.
  Close the parent copy of the child endpoint after successful launch and close
  unrelated child copies before exec. Do not leave duplicate writers that hide
  EOF.
* Accept at most one complete, bounded, valid arm for one launch/session.
  Latch consumption before side effects. Reject repeats, replay on a duplicate
  descriptor, stale launch/session, malformed/truncated input, wrong endpoint
  type, extra descriptors, unauthorized sender, and timeout/EOF before a valid
  arm. Never retry a rejected arm as a new authorization.
* Close the arm input after consumption or rejection. On teardown, close every
  owned endpoint once. Child exit or EOF revokes the transient channel; it does
  not restore or transfer authority.
* Do not begin observation or emit a success stream before arm validation and
  independent live-pin resolution. Do not let the arm IDs substitute for that
  resolution.
* Keep the output writer lifecycle separate. A later complete stream requires
  all expected records, including the separately produced fourth receipt, and
  closure of every writer so the collector sees EOF. A retained duplicate writer
  must produce bounded timeout, not success.
