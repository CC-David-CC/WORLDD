Automatic supervisor crash fencing
Status: Missing behavior reproduced. Candidate01 is implemented. Qualification is running.
Production integration remains pending.

Invariant

An owning supervisor requests an exact issuer fence after unexpected loss of its started authority service.
It preserves outstanding obligations and records checked cleanup separately.

Triggers and exclusions

Handle unexpected child death and loss of the private management parent.
Also handle termination signals and fatal participant-channel failures after an owned start.
Use retained launch ownership and authorization. Do not accept a caller-supplied generation.
Do not infer successful activation from the previous_generation counter.
Require the issuer to verify the exact consumed authorization, as the explicit fence operation does.

Keep graceful release and ordinary explicit stop behavior unchanged.
Do not repeat an explicit fence operation merely because its failure reaches the outer error handler.
Do not fence a generation whose checked graceful release already completed.
Do not fence an unstarted launch or reconstruct ownership from a reopened journal.
An interrupted startup may have an unconsumed authorization. Preserve its unresolved status accurately.

Failure ordering

Stop private participant forwarding before attempting the issuer fence.
Use the existing checked IssuerClient and retained supervisor key.
Use bounded transport deadlines. Do not add automatic reconnect or unbounded retries.
Persist an exact checked fence when the issuer confirms it.
Attempt existing checked process and native cleanup even if fencing fails.
Preserve the original fatal error and the nonzero supervisor exit status.
Report a fencing failure separately. Do not report issuer retirement or completed recovery.
Preserve a committed fence when later cleanup fails.
Preserve unknown cleanup when receipts cannot be verified.

Required evidence

Reproduce child-death and management-loss failures against the unchanged supervisor candidate.
Use real activated children, the production supervisor, real issuer state, and checked native/process cleanup.
Keep a pending revocation obligation through both crashes.
Verify the complete returned fence binding and unchanged activation history after implementation.
Verify graceful release, unconsumed authorization, explicit stop, and explicit fence failure paths.
Verify issuer unavailability, signing-key replacement, native cleanup uncertainty, and local journal failure.
Run focused and cumulative checks in Debug and runtime ASan/UBSan on both hosts.
Keep existing test assertions and locked files unchanged.

Limits

This increment does not implement dependent application-control cleanup or verified issuer retirement.
It does not recover a dead supervisor's lost ownership handles.
It does not authorize replacement execution.
M02 and Spatial-M1 remain incomplete.
