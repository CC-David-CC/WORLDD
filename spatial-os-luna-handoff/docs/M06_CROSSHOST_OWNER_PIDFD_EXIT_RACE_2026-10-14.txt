## Guard invariant and change

The complete recorded-identity match remains the only live-process acceptance path. On identity mismatch or read failure, the guard checks its already-open pidfd. Only POLLIN or POLLHUP readiness proves exit; POLLERR and POLLNVAL do not. The current `/proc` start tick must not identify a different live PID instance. An unconfirmed identity-read failure raises an error. A live identity mismatch remains an error. The guard closes its pidfd exactly once on every exception path. Callers already treat `ProcessLookupError` as an exited child and do not signal it.
