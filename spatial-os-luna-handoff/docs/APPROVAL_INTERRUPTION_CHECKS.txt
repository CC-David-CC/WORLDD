Supervisor approval interruption checks

Invariant
Remote approval alone must not permit child execution.
Require a live participant and a durable local approval record.
After interrupted approval, report failure and stop the exact owned process and native scopes.
Do not retry an uncertain approval or replay execution.
A fresh launch requires a new journal, incarnation, authorization, process scope, and native credential.
An unactivated old approval must not become a new launch's authority.

Scenarios
Use two TLS relays that forward actual issuer requests and replies.
The real issuer signs proofs, commits approvals, and maintains activation state.
Inject faults after approval commit:
1. Close the participant transport while holding the supervisor's committed approval reply.
2. Withhold a participant revocation reply until its unchanged 700 ms fixture deadline expires.
3. Drop the supervisor approval reply after the issuer commits it.
Verify no child configuration, execution marker, stdout, or activation appears.
Verify the issuer's committed unactivated approval and the exact checked local cleanup independently.
Verify a subsequent fresh launch cannot reuse the abandoned approval.

Test timing
Keep the participant and issuer fixture deadlines unchanged.
Use a 3000 ms supervisor observer deadline in these new fault scenarios.
It must outlive the participant's 700 ms deadline to observe that failure during approval.
This does not change a production default or an existing acceptance deadline.

Selection
First run the three scenarios with verified existing Debug and sanitizer binaries.
Then register authority-approval-interruption in CMake and rebuild required executables.
Run the new suite with authority-supervisor, authority-owned-participant,
authority-control-binding, and identity-supervisor-registry in both profiles.
Preserve all failed evidence. Keep locked acceptance files unchanged.

Limits

Recovery failure races, cross-host cancellation, and the full M02 gate remain pending.
