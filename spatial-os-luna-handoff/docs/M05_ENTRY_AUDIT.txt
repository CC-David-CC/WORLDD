Existing production foundations inspected

src/world/store.cpp creates a random persistent world ID and an entry room in SQLite.
Restart reads that identity from the metadata table.
proto/spatial/identity/v1/world_session.proto separates admission configuration from actor proof and grants.
WorldIdentityConfig supports registered-actor and explicit-actor admission.
WorldActorTlsConfig supplies the real remote transport configuration.
tests/core/test_world_remote.py exercises TLS actor channels and admission separately from valid identity.
Its existing remote fixture and related security/lifecycle suites do not prove the complete two-host M05 topology.
The M03 cross-host cancellation matrix proves cancellation across independent worlds, not one shared persistent world.

Scope boundaries

World transitions and per-world workspace selection belong to M06.
Entry must not create, join, or select a workspace implicitly.
Remote application streaming and input remain outside M1.
The shared-world witness must use real geometry, actors, placements, and authority over the production transport.
M1 does not require replicas, consensus, automatic failover, or workspace migration.
