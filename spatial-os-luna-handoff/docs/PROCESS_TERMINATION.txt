Required invariant
Successful cancellation requires confirmed namespace-leader exit and successful reap of the owned outer child.
A denied signal, failed wait, or cleanup deadline cannot produce cancellation success.
Retain uncertainty when cleanup fails. The broker must not acknowledge a revocation barrier after failed cleanup.

Isolated correction
Hold Bubblewrap before application execution with its existing block-fd option.
Pin the direct child with pidfd_open while startup remains held.
Check the direct parent and nested PID-namespace leader identity through proc status.
Use the pinned handle for termination. Never signal a guessed or reused PID.
Wait for that namespace leader to exit. Then check waitpid for the owned outer process.
Use bounded cleanup waits. An unconfirmed destructor or constructor rollback stops the broker with an error.
This preserves unresolved issuer obligations once broker participation is integrated.
No protobuf schema changes are needed. Application and fixture commands still use framed protobuf.
