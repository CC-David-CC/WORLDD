M08 required cleanup batches - 2026-10-18

The deployed M08 gate requires both `broker-copy-preprune-cleanup` and
`broker-copy-preprune-deadline-multirow`. They are separate mandatory CTest
registrations. The gate rejects an inventory missing either registration in
Debug or ASan-UBSan before launching qualification.

The deployed-gate harness adds the multirow registration to its existing
parameterized missing-registration test. Its existing complete-inventory
control covers the new requirement through `gate.REQUIRED`. The 89-case
harness inventory and inherited baseline remain unchanged. This inventory
repair does not qualify M08 or replace host runtime evidence.
