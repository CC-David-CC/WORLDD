Contract change
An owner revocation may now remain pending while every active guardian-backed
world generation fences its native seats. The issuer persists that intent and
its exact generation obligations. It commits the revocation and creates the
existing postcommit cancellation barrier only after all receipts are stored.
This keeps the postcommit barrier as a separate settlement proof.

Only the retained supervisor may sign a native fence receipt. The issuer checks
that signature against the registered supervisor key. The world child cannot
set the guardian activation flag or submit a fence acknowledgment. A stopped or
unavailable guardian leaves the owner operation pending and blocks activation
or release of the obligated generation.
