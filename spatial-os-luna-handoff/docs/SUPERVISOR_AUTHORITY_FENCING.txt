Supervisor authority fencing
Status: Qualified, integrated at a3a926d, and adopted on both hosts. Complete recovery remains pending.

Invariant

The owning supervisor fences only its exact authorized launch before reporting successful fenced cleanup.
Fencing does not retire the issuer generation or complete its outstanding obligations.

Protocol addition

Add AuthorityLaunchRecord.fence at field 10, with type spatial.identity.v1.AuthorityFenceState.
Add AuthorityLaunchRequest.fence at field 20, with type AuthorityLaunchQuery.
Keep every existing field number, enum value, and locked test unchanged.
The private management request supplies no participant, generation, launch, key, or cleanup assertion.
Use the authorization configuration retained by the successful production authorize operation.
Do not persist private key paths or issuer configuration paths in the launch journal.

Owned request construction

Require an existing supervised launch and its retained process and native ownership objects.
Require a successful start attempt and the exact durable launch authorization.
Use the durable issuer, participant registration, incarnation, launch ID, authorization ID, and supervisor revision.
The authorization's previous_generation defines the only candidate generation: previous_generation plus one.
Reject integer overflow.
This calculation is a request constraint. It is not evidence that activation occurred.
Require the issuer to verify that this exact authorization was consumed by the exact active generation.
Reject an unconsumed authorization. Do not invent an activation receipt.
Require the signing key to match the public key in the original launch authorization.
Use the checked production IssuerClient and the AUTHORITY_FENCING challenge path.

Local ordering

Stop private participant forwarding before requesting the fence.
Close the child relay and retained service connection. Discard queued participant messages.
Do not resume forwarding after a failed fence attempt.
Compare the returned fence against the entire owned authorization and launch binding.
Persist the checked fence before returning it to the management caller.
A failed journal transaction must not produce a successful fence reply.
The issuer may have committed the fence even when local persistence fails. Report that uncertainty.
A retained coordinator can retry the same request with a fresh proof.
An already persisted matching fence returns the same record without repeating external effects.

The production supervisor handles fence by requesting fencing, then stopping its owned process and native scopes.
Return the record only after both checked cleanup receipts exist.
Exit after draining that response. Do not return to child routing.
On fencing failure, attempt the existing checked cleanup and return an error.
Preserve a successful issuer fence even if subsequent native or process cleanup fails.
Keep ordinary inspect, stop, graceful release, and crash handling unchanged in this increment.
Automatic crash fencing and verified issuer retirement remain required recovery work.

Persistence

Add one optional field to the version 2 supervised launch record.
The SQLite table and user_version remain unchanged.
Old records without this field retain their existing meaning.
Validate every field of a persisted fence against its original launch authorization on read.
A forged or mismatched journal field must prevent startup.
Reopening a record does not reconstruct ownership, reload signing configuration, or authorize new execution.
No operator database migration is authorized by this increment.

Use a real production supervisor and a real activated child, not a caller-supplied activation report.
Verify exact issuer fencing, checked process/native cleanup, and a matching durable launch record.
Verify that issuer activation and pending revocation obligations remain unresolved.
Reject fencing before activation and after graceful issuer release.
Reject changed signing material and mismatched durable fence identities.

Run focused and full qualification in Debug and runtime ASan/UBSan on both hosts.

Remaining recovery scope

This operation does not prove dependent application-control reservation cleanup.
It does not issue a verified retirement receipt or authorize replacement execution.
Automatic crash coordination, supervisor restart recovery, issuer retirement, and cross-host cancellation remain required.
