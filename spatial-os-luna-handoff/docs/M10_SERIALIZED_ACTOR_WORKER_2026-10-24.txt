Capture lifetime
A capture binding fixes actor generation, capture generation, and the exact interruption handle.
Only the worker retires that binding. Receivers retain and observe the same binding object.
Stale input cannot enqueue urgency, cancel an adopted actor, or select a new interruption handle.
Current malformed input and queue overflow retire their binding and request ordinary actor cancellation.
Checked cancellation does not grant, revoke, or replace ordinary actor authority.
The worker publishes one flight containing its context, timestamp, serial, and urgency.
Its decision and interruption request share the flight completion barrier.
It never takes actor or controller locks.
An interruption request is not a checked cancellation or cleanup receipt.
Unexpected worker termination interrupts its exact actor and exposes a terminal failure flag.
The idle root checks that flag every 100 ms. Partial framing may delay observation until its ten-second deadline.
Actor interruption does not wait for that root observation.
Uncertain native output closes the affected actor transport. Existing cleanup obligations remain retained.
