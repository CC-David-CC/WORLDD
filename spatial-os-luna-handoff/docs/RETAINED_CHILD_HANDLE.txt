Retained child handle: coordinator integration prerequisite
Status: The development branch implements the private handle server. Coordinator reception is implemented through ChildHandleClient.

Observed source

ProcessScope retains child_ and pidfd_ privately.
Its public interface now duplicates the retained live pidfd after process-lifetime checks.
ProcessScopeState contains no direct child PID or process descriptor.
AuthorityLaunch retains ProcessScope through its private implementation.
AuthorityLaunchRecord contains durable scope identity, not live kernel handles.
The supervisor management stream still carries framed protobuf bytes without ancillary descriptors.
A separate optional private socket transfers broker handles. Read CHILD_HANDLE_PROTOCOL.txt for the additive contract.

Required behavior

The coordinator must authenticate replies from the exact supervised broker child.
The administrative socket creator is the coordinator, not that child.
A journal record cannot supply a live process handle.
Do not replace the retained child identity with PID discovery or command-name matching.
Do not accept the first administrative sender as proof of ownership.

Next production interface

Provide a narrowly scoped live child-handle query from the retained supervisor.
Return a duplicate of the existing pidfd, not a newly opened PID lookup.
Correlate the transfer with the exact launch and private parent request.
Require a started, authorized, live launch with retained process ownership.
Reject reopened journals, stopped scopes, failed authorization, and unrelated launch identities.
Use a descriptor-capable private channel with bounded framing and credential checks.
Close received descriptors on every rejected or uncertain reply.
Keep the original supervisor pidfd until checked process teardown completes.
Do not give this descriptor to worlds, actors, or workspace applications.

The additive protobuf messages and private socket contract are recorded in CHILD_HANDLE_PROTOCOL.txt.
Keep all locked acceptance files unchanged unless a separate reviewed contract change requires otherwise.

Independent acceptance

Reject another launch's handle, a dead handle, ordinary files, and duplicated reply descriptors.
