Authority fencing
Status: Proposed additive contract. Production fencing is not implemented.

Invariant

Only the registered supervisor can durably fence its exact active, previously authorized authority launch.
Fencing blocks service acknowledgements, service release, and replacement activation.
Fencing alone never completes a revocation obligation.

Relationship to recovery

Use fencing before accepting a supervisor cleanup receipt or authorizing replacement execution.
Local crash cleanup can happen before the issuer becomes reachable.
Such cleanup does not authorize retirement until the exact generation is fenced and its receipt is verified.
Keep the activation active and preserve its applied_revision until separate checked retirement is implemented.
Never infer external cleanup from fencing, process exit, a timeout, or an issuer signature.

Validation and proof

Require a real TLS connection binding before creating or completing a challenge.
Require 32-byte participant, incarnation, launch, and authorization identifiers.
Require positive generation and revision values within the signed SQLite integer range.
Match the complete registered participant record and current supervisor binding.
Match the exact active activation and its consumed durable launch authorization.
Verify service incarnation, generation, authorization ID, launch ID, supervisor revision, and supervisor key together.
Reject unsupervised activations and inactive generations.
Do not require the old activation's issuer incarnation to equal the current issuer incarnation.
Recovery must remain possible after an issuer restart.

Use audience identityd/authority-fence/v1 and signature purpose AUTHORITY_FENCING.
Bind the proof to a fresh nonce, exact request, issuer incarnation, and requesting TLS connection.
Consume a nonce on the first completion attempt.
Reject expired proofs, wrong keys, wrong purposes, cross-connection proofs, and replay.
Revalidate all mutable authority context inside the completion transaction.
An owner key or participant key cannot substitute for the registered supervisor key.

Durable state and service behavior

Add schema version 9 through the explicit --migrate path.
Create authority_fence with a primary key on participant_id and generation.
Reference the exact authority_activation row with a foreign key.
Store the canonical request, fence ID, fence security revision, supervisor key, and canonical state.
Require unique fence IDs and consistent serialized and indexed identities on read.
Keep activation rows, applied revisions, obligations, launch authorization, and supervisor binding unchanged during fencing.
Commit one fence atomically. A failed insert changes no durable state.
An identical request with a fresh valid proof returns the same committed fence.
Conflicting requests never replace that fence.

Reject applied and release requests for the fenced generation, including previously valid retries.
Keep read-only authority-state inspection available.
Block new launch authorization and activation while the fenced active generation remains unresolved.
Old or second service sockets cannot bypass the generation gate.
Keep pre-existing barriers pending where the fenced service had not acknowledged them.
New revocations still include the fenced active generation.
Do not change the original applied_revision to represent supervisor action.
