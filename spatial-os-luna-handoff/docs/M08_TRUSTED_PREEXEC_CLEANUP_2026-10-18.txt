# Trusted pre-exec cleanup proof (2026-10-18)

A released startup gate remains conservatively marked as possibly executable. A complete positive native errno record followed by EOF from the private close-on-exec gate proves the trusted child failed before the requested executable ran. This separate in-process proof allows checked cleanup of an unbound broker scope after a failed exec.

The gate reader uses one absolute two-second deadline. It accepts only exactly one Linux errno value in the range 1 through 4095, followed by EOF. Successful exec closes the gate without a record. Partial, oversized, zero, out-of-range, unreadable, or late acknowledgements remain ambiguous and cannot produce the proof. External payload attachment clears the proof before attachment work.

The broker cleanup participant checks the exact retained root identity first. It permits unbound cleanup only when execution was impossible or the trusted pre-exec proof exists. Normal drain, reap, identity verification, and stop-receipt handling remain in force.

Focused core regressions cover missing-executable cleanup, successful-exec rejection, short, oversized, and zero acknowledgements, a late acknowledgement under repeated signal interruptions, and retained-scope recovery. Runtime qualification is pending primary review.
