Supervisor-owned participant connection
Status: isolated candidate01 passed 339 focused Debug checks on both hosts. Sanitizer qualification is running.

Invariant

A supervised launch cannot execute until its retained participant connection has durable, exact supervisor approval.
The coordinator constructs that connection. It does not accept caller-supplied authentication results.
The coordinator retains the process and native scope before authentication or approval.

Ownership

AuthorityLaunch owns the ParticipantRuntime worker in the trusted supervisor process.
The worker authenticates through the existing checked issuer client.
Its configured issuer, participant, registration revision, and explicit service incarnation must match the launch binding.
Its registered domains must match the configured domain set.
The supervisor private key remains outside the child and workspace boundaries.
The service connection stays with the coordinator for activation, revocation acknowledgement, and release.
The authority child must later use a private inherited participant channel.
That child-channel integration is a separate remaining requirement, not an alternate authority path.

All existing fields keep their numbers and meanings.
These are private supervisor operations. They grant no actor execution access.

Persistence

Existing unsupervised records retain record schema version 1.
New supervised records use record schema version 2.
The SQLite container remains version 1. Existing records are not silently converted.
Record validation requires the matching supervised flag and record version.
The exact approval must match the record binding and retained process launch identity.
Persist approval before START_ATTEMPTED and before child execution.
Do not persist configuration paths, credentials, signing keys, or native cookies.
Issuer approval and journal persistence are separate transactions.
A lost reply or failed journal write does not permit child execution.
An exact retry may retrieve the same issuer approval through the existing checked protocol.
Restart cannot reconstruct a live participant connection from a saved record.
Do not replay a launch or reuse its service connection identity after restart.

Live operations

Attach only once, while the supervised launch is prepared and retains both owned scopes.
Validate the runtime configuration against the binding before opening its issuer connection.
Consume authenticated updates only from the retained worker.
Reject identity mismatch and permanent worker failure.
Construct the approval request from the retained worker identity and durable launch record.
Sign only the checked issuer challenge for that exact request.
Store the checked approval before allowing start.
Reject start without approval, after cleanup starts, after worker failure, or without retained scopes.
Accept participant commands only after the owned launch starts.
Use the existing worker validation and issuer checks for every command.
Closing the worker does not retire its issuer generation or clear revocation obligations.
