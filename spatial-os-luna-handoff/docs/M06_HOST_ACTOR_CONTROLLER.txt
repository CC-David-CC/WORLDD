HostActorController owns its active ActorSessionClient. Trusted setup registers retained
world owners and fresh actor-channel factories. Registration may include the starting
world. A transition to the current world remains invalid. Registration does not admit
an actor, grant authority, attach a presentation, or select a foreground world.

The production constructor uses SessionCoordinator::inspect_presentation and
SessionCoordinator::select_world. Selection follows confirmed destination activation.
The controller never detaches, retires, or stops a world.

current_client() is removed. The actor path forwards only semantic inspect and action
operations. Each call carries the context generation. A successful transition or a
preparation attempt advances the generation; callers must adopt the returned generation.
Invalid requests and busy operations do not advance it. Old generations reject locally.
The controller holds one mutex through bounded client exchanges and host selection.
Concurrent inspect, action, and transition calls reject while another operation owns it.
Trusted factories and presentation callbacks must remain bounded and must not reenter
the controller. They are not exposed to actors. Status getters acquire the same mutex.
The fixture constructor remains a trusted test seam, not an actor action interface.

Request validation calls existing validate_message before effects. Unknown fields,
nested unknown fields, nonfinite values, and malformed poses reject locally.
Unsupported versions retain UNSUPPORTED_VERSION. HostActorTransitionResult appends
request_error as field 7. Actor/server result facts remain in actor_transition.
This new draft schema addition does not change locked acceptance tests or contracts.
All throwing setup precedes the in-flight guard. A scope guard clears transitioning_
on every exit. Exceptions after effects begin leave control fenced without replay.

Preparation denial preserves source semantic access and leaves selection unchanged.
Matched server errors preserve their typed error and existing uncertainty.
Uncertain departure or activation fences control. Confirmed activation with uncertain
selection retains a quarantined destination channel and separate actor/host facts.
The controller does not retry, reconnect, or replay. Channels close on destruction.
Probe scopes destroy controllers before joining EOF witnesses.
